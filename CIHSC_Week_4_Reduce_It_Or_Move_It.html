Reduce it, or move it?

“I want my breathing settled enough to get out and do ordinary things again — see my sister, get to the shops without planning it like an expedition. I need the damp sorted and I need to know what money I’ve got coming in. And I can’t keep spending so much of my week getting to appointments.” — Mr H
Your task: Each card is a real response somebody might propose for Mr H. Sort each one by what it does to the work in his life. Then press Compare to see how we would sort it and, more importantly, the condition each one depends on. There are no scores here and no right totals: most of these can go either way, and saying what it depends on is the whole skill. On a touchscreen, tap a card then tap a column.
