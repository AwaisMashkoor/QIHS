Why is Sarah unwell?

Sarah, 52

Sarah has COPD and type 2 diabetes. She has smoked since she was 16. She lives alone and has missed some clinic appointments. Her practice nurse describes her as "not engaging with her care plan".

Your task: Drag the factors from the left panel onto the chain to build your explanation of why Sarah is unwell. Start with what you can see. Then toggle the Wider determinants lens to reveal what a clinical view does not show, and place those too. When ready, click Show analysis to compare your explanation with the determinants analysis and see what changed. On a touchscreen, tap a factor then tap where it goes.
