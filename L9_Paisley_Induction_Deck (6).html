CHSC Level 9 Induction · The Essentials

How assessment works

First attempt

  • Submit on time — full mark
  • Request an extension before the deadline — full mark
  • Submit up to a week late, no extension — mark minus 10
  • More than a week late — not marked
  • Extenuating circumstances — submit at next attempt, uncapped

Second and third attempts

  • Next term of study, including summer
  • Mark is capped at the pass mark

You need to pass every Level 9 module to go on to honours.

CHSC Level 9 Induction · The Essentials

AI and your own work

✅

Fine, if declared

  • Checking spelling and grammar
  • Finding journals and reading lists
  • Organising your own notes and ideas
🚫

Not fine

  • Generating the content you submit
  • Paraphrasing tools to hide sources
  • Declaring you didn't use AI when you did

Every submission has a declaration cover page. Say what you used. A false declaration is academic misconduct.

CHSC Level 9 Induction · The Essentials

True or false?

Vote by show of hands first — then we'll reveal.

If I miss a deadline, I can email for an extension the day after.
Extensions must be requested before the deadline. After it, the 10-mark late penalty applies for one week, then it isn't marked.
A second attempt is capped at the pass mark.
Yes. Second and third attempts pass, but the recorded mark is capped.
Using Grammarly means I should tick "no AI used" to be safe.
The opposite. Declare it. Grammar checking is fine when declared; a false declaration is misconduct.
I need to pass all Level 9 modules to progress to honours.
Every Level 9 module must be passed before you move to Level 10.

Real situations. What would you do?

📱 Turnitin trouble

It's Sunday evening. Your assessment is due tomorrow and Turnitin won't accept the file.

👆 Tap to reveal
Screenshot the error with the time. Email the module coordinator straight away — evidence of a submission attempt before the deadline matters. Then IT support.

📉 Falling behind

You've missed two weeks because you were unwell and you're worried about catching up.

👆 Tap to reveal
Tell your module tutor now, not later. Ask about extenuating circumstances if it's affecting deadlines. Your personal tutor can help you plan.

❓ Feedback confusion

You've had a mark back and you don't understand the feedback.

👆 Tap to reveal
Book a conversation with the person who marked it. Feedback is meant to be used on the next piece — ask what one thing would move the mark most.

💭 Things are difficult

Something outside university is starting to affect your studies.

👆 Tap to reveal
Personal tutor first. Student Services for wellbeing, funding, disability support. You don't have to explain the detail to ask for help.

What changes at Level 9

📐

From describing to evaluating

Level 8 asked what. Level 9 asks how well, compared with what, and says who. Every claim needs evidence; every source gets questioned.

📊

Three modules at once

At least six assessments across the term. The people who struggle are the ones who discover two deadlines in the same week in November.

🎓

The door to honours

Pass every Level 9 module and Level 10 — including your dissertation — opens. Start thinking about what you'd want to research.

Critical Research Appraisal is built exactly for the first of these. Treat it as the module that makes the other five easier.
