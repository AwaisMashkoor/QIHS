How many are multimorbid?

Multimorbidity means having two or more conditions from a list. Here is the catch: the answer depends on how long the list is.

One study. 1,168,620 people in England. The researchers made a list of 80 conditions, ranked from commonest to rarest, and asked the same question again and again: how many of these people have two or more conditions from the list? First with a list of only 2. Then 10. Then all 80.
How long is the list?Same 1,168,620 people every time. Only the length of the list changes.
